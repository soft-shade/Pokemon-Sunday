Pokémon Sunday Version is a classic style Pokémon game set in the Kiraki Region using gen 3 style tilesets and the full National Dex through Gen 9 (1,046 Pokémon, including the new Legends Z-A Megas), plus 23 Fakémon and 11 new forms of existing Pokémon.

The Gen 9 update completely rebuilds the game on Pokémon Essentials v21.1. Pokémon now follow you in the overworld, and the game is playable in 6 languages: English, French, German, Spanish, Italian and Japanese.

It is a completed game with a complex plot, intricate maps, and 8 fun gyms with puzzles that include a matching game, dodging ninjas, flooding or unflooding pools in the proper order, warp tiles, regional trivia and more.

A complex battle tower with 1390 hand-built competitive Smogon sets and 4 tiers of Pokémon for battling. Choose 3v3, 6v6 or Doubles at Basic, Normal, Competitive, or Ultra Tier, or try the new rental mode!

There are also regional variants of the Sentret line, Taillow line, and Skarmory, plus a Kiraki Vivillon pattern.
Rotom and Castform gain new forms (including the new Locust Castform), and there is a Flygon Mega evolution.

Weak to Dark, Dragon.

Immune to Fire.

33 new, completely animated moves (mostly Light-type)

An original soundtrack for the overworld Routes and Towns, and the Intro music is created by Jeff Clements.

The player can become a member of the Elite Four or Champion after playing through the main story line, and be challenged (and possibly replaced!) by many of the strong trainers and gym leaders of the region.  The elite four will change over time as challengers take down current members.

Get a special punch on your trainer card for every same type team of Pokémon that you use to successfully become Champion!

Plot:

[SPOILER]

The player washes up on the shores of the Igandea River near Germanium Village with no memory of who they are or how they got there.  The local Pokémon researcher, Professor Cherry suggests visiting the gym leaders of nearby towns. As they are community leaders, he suggests they may recognize you. The player journeys around, meeting the gym leaders, and slowly realizing that while some people recognize them, they seem hesitant to help the player out.
The player goes on a journey of rooting out corruption as they try to discover their identity.
[/SPOILER]

Non Standard Mechanics:
[SPOILER]
Moves which are supereffective against an opponent's primary type do 2.1X damage, vs 1.9X damage against the secondary type
Similarly, Not very effective moves on primary type do 0.45X damage, whereas if NVE on secondary type, does 0.55X damage
Evolutions:
Inkay-> Malamer: Level 30, no upside down needed
Meltan->Melmetal: Level 50
Phione->Manaphy Level 50
Galar Farfetchd->Sirfetch'd: Level while holding Leek (plan to change to standard, code just isn't working atm)
Galar Yamask->Runerigus: Same method as canon, just go under Precious Metal bridge after taking 49 damage
Milcery->Alcremie: Level 25. Toppings chosen at will in the museum
Rockruff-> Dusk Lycanrok: Does not need Own Tempo, any Rockruff can evolve to Dusk form if leveled at dusk
Trade evolutions with no item (e.g. Machoke->Machamp): NPCs around the world will be looking to trade with you
Trade evolutions with an item (e.g. Rhydon with protector) Can be done in the museum
Location Evolutions:
    -Mossy stone-Mt. Silicon Forest
    -Icy Stone/Mt Lanakila- Mt. Silicon
    -Magnetic Fields- Iron Town
Fossil resurrection: Each Galar fossil has been remapped to one Pokémon instead of needing a pair. Collect in Iron Town. Resurrect in the museum.

Breeding: Either parent with Hidden Ability has a 40% chance to pass it on. If both parents have HA it is 100%.
Gender neutral Pokémon can breed with gender neutral Pokémon in the same egg group.
Shiny Pokémon have 1/40 chance of being passed on if neither parent is Ditto. Shiny Charm increases odds to 1/20.
Flame body/armor stack additively to increase hatch rate.
